# Agent

## Open this page

Choose **Ask Back Office** at the bottom right to open the assistant beside your
work. Choose **Agent** in the navigation, or **Expand** in the rail, for a larger
conversation with a session explorer. **Minimize to rail** returns to your previous page
and keeps the conversation open in the rail.

## What you can do

Ask about cases and missing requirements, continue earlier conversations, and
choose an available model. The assistant uses the Back Office Agent configured
for this application's environment.

## Common tasks

Open the person or document you are working on, then open the assistant. Its
context line shows the record references included with your next message. Ask
which requirements remain or how to interpret the evidence. The initial agent
can query cases, document sets and missing requirements. Uploading, editing,
extraction and document review remain in the application screens.

The assistant retrieves records under your permissions. A record reference does
not grant access. Verify its explanation against the current case records before
acting; the backend determines requirement status.

## Understand what you see

- Drag the rail's left edge to resize it, or focus **Resize chat rail** and use
  the arrow keys. Shift with an arrow makes larger changes. Home and End choose
  the minimum and maximum widths; double-click resets the width.
- Use the expanded page's session explorer to open a previous conversation.
- Choose a model when prompted. The picker can open model-provider settings if
  you need to connect a provider. **Back to conversation** returns to the thread.
- Closing the rail hides it. Use the conversation's **Stop session** action to
  stop a response in progress.

## If something goes wrong

If the assistant is unavailable, the agent may still be deploying or you may
lack access. Choose **Retry assistant connection** after the issue is resolved.
You can continue using the Back Office screens while the assistant is unavailable.
If the model or agent cannot respond, the conversation shows its error and retry
options. An unavailable response does not mean that the case has no missing evidence.
