# User guide

Back Office helps you collect a person's documents, compare their attributes with
reference information, and understand which assigned requirements still need work.
Open Back Office from the Command Center navigation menu and sign in with an
account that has access. You can work on your own cases; authorized reviewers
can also inspect cases and record document decisions.

## Find a feature

- [People](persons/index.md): create reference profiles and assign requirements.
- [Documents](documents/index.md): upload evidence, complete attributes, and review revisions.
- [Document Sets](document-sets/index.md): inspect published requirements and accepted alternatives.
- [Reconciliation](reconciliation/index.md): evaluate a case and follow its findings.
- [Agent](agent/index.md): ask the Back Office assistant about requirements and missing evidence.
- [Admin](admin/index.md): organization administrators configure classification-confidence acceptance by document type.

The initial new-account requirements are an illustrative policy. Confirm that
the policy is appropriate for your organization before relying on its results.
Acceptance records a review decision; it does not establish document authenticity.

If a change is interrupted, use **Check again** or **Resume and reload** in the
interrupted-change notice. Avoid starting a different change until the recorded
result is known. Unknown profile details can remain empty; do not invent values.
